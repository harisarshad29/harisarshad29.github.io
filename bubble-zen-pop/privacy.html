Bubble Zen Pop — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Developer: Harris Games ("we", "us") · Contact: haris.arshad.29@gmail.com

Bubble Zen Pop is an offline-first puzzle game for adults. It has no accounts, chat or social features, and we do not ask you for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Data stored only on your device

The game keeps a save file on your device with your level progress, stars, scores, coins, boosters, lives timer, settings (sound, music, vibration, colour-blind symbols, screen shake), daily-reward and event progress, ad-frequency timestamps, and a record of purchase transaction IDs so a purchase is never granted twice. This file never leaves your device through our code. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). Before any ad is requested, the game asks for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and certain US states) using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options.

According to Google, the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • App interactions such as app launches, taps and ad video views;
  • Diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate and SDK performance;
  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google serves non-personalised or limited ads according to your choice. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. If you buy Remove Ads, banner and interstitial ads stop; optional rewarded ads stay available if you want them.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing via Unity IAP)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card or billing details. The game receives a purchase confirmation (product ID, transaction ID and receipt) so it can unlock what you bought. Purchases are handled through Unity's In-App Purchasing library; see Unity's privacy policy at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

4. Unity engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. We have not enabled Unity Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics or other optional Unity services.

5. Children

Bubble Zen Pop is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected through an ad partner, contact us and we will help you request deletion from that partner.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Ad consent: Settings → Privacy options (shown where applicable).
  • Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Local data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of personal data. Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; contact us and we will help.

7. Security and retention

We do not run servers that store your data. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

8. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if the game's data practices change (for example if a new SDK is added).

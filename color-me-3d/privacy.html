Color Me 3D — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Developer: Harris Games ("we", "us") · Contact: haris.arshad.29@gmail.com

Color Me 3D is a relaxing 3D paint-by-number game. It has no accounts, chat or social features, and we do not ask you for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Data stored only on your device

The game keeps data on your device: your coloring progress, finished characters in your gallery, coins, unlocked levels, daily challenge and daily reward progress, and settings (music, sound, vibration). This data never leaves your device through our code. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). Before any ad is requested, the game asks for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and certain US states) using Google's User Messaging Platform. Where it applies, you can change your choice later from the game's Settings → Privacy options.

According to Google, the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • App interactions such as app launches, taps and ad video views;
  • Diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate and SDK performance;
  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID, or on iPhone and iPad the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA).

This data is encrypted in transit. If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google serves non-personalised or limited ads according to your choice. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. On iPhone and iPad the game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency question once; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared, and you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. The game has no in-app purchases.

3. Unity engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. We have not enabled Unity Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics or other optional Unity services.

4. Children

Color Me 3D is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected through an ad partner, contact us and we will help you request deletion from that partner.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Ad consent: Settings → Privacy options (shown where applicable).
  • Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iPhone/iPad, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Local data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android settings (on iPhone/iPad, deleting the app removes its data).
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of personal data. Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; contact us and we will help.

6. Security and retention

We do not run servers that store your data. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

7. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if the game's data practices change (for example if a new SDK is added).

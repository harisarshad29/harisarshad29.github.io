Unofficial GTA 6 Companion

Privacy Policy

The Unofficial GTA 6 Companion works without an account. This privacy policy applies to the companion app.

Data the app stores

The app saves your notes, bookmarks, checklist items and completion status on your device. The app uses these data to restore your saved items when you reopen it. The developer does not receive these data. The app does not use advertising, tracking, analytics or cloud synchronization.

Links to other websites

When you choose a source or map link, the app opens that website in your browser. The companion does not attach your saved items or notes to those links. Your browser and the website you visit handle that traffic under their own privacy settings and policies.

Deletion

In Favorites, choose Clear all local data to reset your checklist and remove your notes and bookmarks. The app itself does not have an account or a remote copy of your saved data.

Support

Contact Harris Games for questions about the app or this policy.
